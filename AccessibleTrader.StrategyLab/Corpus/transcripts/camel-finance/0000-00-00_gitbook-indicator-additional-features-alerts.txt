# Alerts
Stay Ahead of the Market with Real-Time Notifications
Our alert system ensures you never miss a key market signal. Whether you’re tracking Daily or Weekly Cycle Lows, you’ll get real-time alerts on TradingView that align with the most critical moments for potential entries, exits, and trend shifts.
***
#### 📣 What You’ll Be Alerted About
**🟢 Daily Cycle Low (DCL) Alerts**
* ✅ New DCL: Get notified when a potential Daily Cycle Low is confirmed — ideal for timing swing entries.
* ❌ Cancelled DCL: Stay updated if a previously confirmed DCL is broken or invalidated.
* 🔄 Updated DCL: Be alerted when a new DCL replaces a failed one — the indicator auto-adjusts.
* ⚠️ Pending DCL: For fine-tuned assets only — an early heads-up that a DCL may be forming soon.

#### ⚙️ Easy Setup on TradingView
* Apply the indicator to any asset
* Enable alerts directly through the TradingView alert icon
* Choose how you want to be notified: in-app, email, sound, or webhook
* Alerts come pre-loaded with asset, price, and timeframe — no setup required
***
#### 🔒 Designed for All Traders
* Free TradingView Users: Set alerts manually for individual assets
* Pro Users: Set alerts on multiple assets across timeframes for maximum coverage
***
#### 🧠 Why It Matters
These alerts act like a personal trading assistant — notifying you only when real, high-probability signals emerge. Perfect for those who don’t want to sit at their charts all day but still want to trade with timing and confidence.
