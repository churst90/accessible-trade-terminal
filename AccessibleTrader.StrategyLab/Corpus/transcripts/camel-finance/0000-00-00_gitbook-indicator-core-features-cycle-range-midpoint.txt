# Cycle Range Midpoint
Pinpoint the Heart of Each Market Cycle
The Cycle Range Midpoint is a precision tool designed to help traders quickly identify the exact centre between two confirmed cycle lows — whether Daily (DCL), Weekly (WCL), or Yearly (YCL).
***
#### ⚙️ Cycle Range Midpoint Settings
Tailor Your Chart, Highlight What Matters
**🟡 Line Display**
* Toggle Visibility: Instantly show or hide the midpoint line
* Line Style: Choose between solid, dashed, or dotted for clear visual distinction
* Full-Length Option: Stretch the midpoint across the entire chart to make key areas easier to monitor
**🟠 Width and Thickness**
* Fine-tune how prominent your midpoint appears — from subtle guidance to bold emphasis
**🟣 Cycle Range Box**
* Enable a shaded box around the midpoint to visualise the range between cycle lows
* Ideal for spotting time symmetry, cycle rhythm, and internal structure
**🔆 Visibility Slider**
* Customise the opacity of both line and box
* Want it subtle in the background? Dial it down.
* Need high visual impact? Max it out.
