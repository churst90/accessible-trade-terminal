# CF Cycle Marker
Visualize Your Own Market Timing with Full Control
The CF Custom Cycle Marker is a lightweight visual overlay designed for traders who want to define and experiment with their own market cycles. Whether you’re testing a theory, illustrating an idea, or manually syncing with price rhythms, this tool gives you a clean canvas to mark cycle lows and render custom sine waves.
What it does:
* 📍 Marks Custom Lows — Set manual cycle low anchors directly on the chart
* 🌊 Plots Sine Waves — Create visual rhythm between selected cycle lows
* 🧭 Flexible Cycle Spacing — Choose how many bars apart your cycles should stretch
* ✨ Custom Toggles — Enable icons, sine waves, background shading, and even a camel logo 🐪
* 🎨 Styling Freedom — Customize line width, color, fill opacity, and more
> ⚠️ Note: This is a manual visual tool for stylized charting. For automated cycle low detection and live market reads, use the CF Cycle Trading Indicator.
Use this tool to sketch, compare, or communicate cycles in a quick and elegant way — perfect for analysis sessions, screenshots, or strategy building.
