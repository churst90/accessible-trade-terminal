# Asset Cycle Count
Stay in Rhythm with Optimised Market Timing
The Asset Cycle Count table is a smart timing reference built directly into your chart — offering you historical cycle expectations across Daily, Weekly, and Yearly ranges for 16 handpicked, fine-tuned assets.
Rather than guessing how long a cycle *should* last, this tool gives you clarity — helping you trade with data-backed timing confidence.
***
#### ⚙️ How It Works
✅ When the indicator detects you’re on one of the 16 officially optimised assets from the CF Tuned Asset Watchlist, the table automatically appears in the bottom-right of your chart.
It shows:
* 🕒 Daily Cycle Range (in days)
* 📆 Weekly Cycle Range (in weeks)
* 📅 Yearly Cycle Range (in years)
These ranges are derived from historical behaviour, macro cycles, and rhythmical confluence — giving you a highly reliable timing reference.
***
#### 🔍 Why It Matters
This isn’t a cycle detection tool — it’s a cycle expectation calibrator.
Use it to:
* Stay aligned with the typical rhythm of each asset
* Avoid misjudging early or late entries
* Spot potential cycle extensions or compressions
* Cross-check your DCL/WCL counts against expected timeframes
* Confirm your trades are backed by statistical timing, not gut feeling
Whether you’re swing trading BTC, planning mid-term setups on SPX, or tracking gold’s yearly rotations — this simple widget keeps your timing in sync.
***
#### 🧠 Pro Tip
Use the Asset Cycle Count table alongside the main CF Cycle Lows Indicator to:
* Validate your bar counts
* Support early reversal expectations
* Manage timing with sharper precision
This is your timing compass — subtle but powerful.
