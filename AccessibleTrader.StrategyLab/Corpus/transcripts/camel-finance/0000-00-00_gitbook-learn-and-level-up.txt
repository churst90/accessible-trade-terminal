# Learn & Level-Up
Courses and Learning Resources That Help Members Understand the Framework Behind the Product
## Learn & Level-Up
The **Learn & Level-Up** section is the education side of **CF Trading**.
It gives members a structured path to understand cycle trading, improve chart skills, and build stronger market context around the indicator.
{% hint style="info" %}
This section is built to teach the logic behind the signals, not just how to click buttons.
{% endhint %}
### What Is Included
Members get access to course content covering:
* Cycle Trading Fundamentals
* Trendline Analysis
* Macro and Seasonal Cycles
* Key U.S. Economic Indicators
* Hyperwave Theory
* TradingView Setup and Workflow
### Cycle Trading Fundamentals
This is the foundation of the learning library.
It covers the core ideas behind cycle trading, including daily, weekly, and yearly cycles, key terminology, confirmation signals, failures, inversions, timing windows, and risk management.
It is designed to help members understand how cycle structure works before applying it in live charts.
### Trendline Analysis
This module focuses on how to draw and use trendlines properly.
It shows how trendlines support cycle analysis, how breakouts can confirm shifts in structure, and how to use them in real trading conditions with more consistency.
### Macro and Seasonal Cycles
This section expands beyond chart structure and into the larger forces that influence markets.
It covers how macro conditions and seasonal patterns affect assets such as Bitcoin, gold, oil, and equities, helping members align technical setups with broader market context.
### Key U.S. Economic Indicators
This module explains the economic data that often drives major moves in risk assets.
Members learn how to understand CPI, PPI, jobs data, unemployment, and other important releases so they can read the macro backdrop with more confidence.
### Hyperwave Theory
This course explores the structure of parabolic moves and major market extremes.
It helps members recognise Hyperwave phases, understand market psychology during those phases, and compare that framework with other tools such as cycle theory and trend analysis.
### TradingView Guidebook
This part of the library helps members use TradingView more effectively.
It covers chart setup, indicators, alerts, layouts, multi-timeframe workflows, and the practical steps needed to make the platform work smoothly with the CF Cycle Trading Indicator.
### Why It Matters
The goal of **Learn & Level-Up** is to make members more capable, not just more informed.
It gives traders the knowledge needed to understand the framework behind the product, apply it with more confidence, and keep improving over time.
