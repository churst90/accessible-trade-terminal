# Finetuned Asset Detection
Smart Auto-Detection for Optimised Performance
To help you get the most accurate readings and eliminate confusion, the CF Cycle Trading Indicator comes equipped with an intelligent Asset & Timeframe Detection Module — ensuring your chart is properly configured for precision cycle analysis.
***
#### ✅ What It Does
This tool automatically checks your chart setup in real time:
* ✅ Detects if you’re on one of the 16 officially fine-tuned assets (like BTC, SPX, or XAU/USD)
* ✅ Verifies that you’re using the Daily (1D) timeframe — required for full indicator functionality
* 🟢 Green “Finetuned Asset Detected” badge appears when both conditions are met
* 🟡 Yellow/Red warning alerts you if you’re using an unsupported asset or timeframe
* 📋 Optional display of the full supported asset list, helping you stay aligned
No guesswork. No misconfigurations. Just confirmation that you’re on the right path.
