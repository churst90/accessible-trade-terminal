# Daily Cycle Lows
### 🔍 Daily Cycle Lows (DCL)
Pinpoint Precision. Reliable Entries. Visual Confidence.
The Daily Cycle Low (DCL) system is at the core of the CF Cycle Trading Indicator — built to help traders identify the most reliable, high-probability turning points in the market.
Whether you’re trading Bitcoin, the S\&P 500, or Gold, DCLs are your go-to anchor for positioning with confidence in trend reversals and fresh cycle legs.

#### 🌐 DCL Confluence – When Signals Align
\
Looking for stronger entries backed by multiple confirmations? DCL Confluence is your edge.
Confluence is the combined weight of multiple technical signals that support the same Daily Cycle Low. It doesn’t guess — it cross-checks.
What’s included:
* ✅ Breakout Confirmation – Detects clean breakouts aligned with cycle low timing

#### 🧠 Smart Confirmation Logic – The DCL Signal System
\
Cycle trading isn’t always clean. That’s why the indicator doesn’t just guess — it adapts.
Here’s how the system works:
* ✅ Confirmed DCL: Shows a ✅ icon the moment all rules align. That’s your high-confidence trigger.
* 🕓 Unconfirmed DCL: Shows a provisional 🕓 icon — low probability, not ready yet.
* ❌ Cancelled DCL: This shows ❌ when a DCL breaks down post-trigger.
* 🔄 Updated DCL: Shows 🔄 when a new low replaces an invalidated one. Tracks shifting market behaviour in real time.
Every signal is mapped on the chart — so you’re never second-guessing what the market is doing or where you stand in the cycle.
