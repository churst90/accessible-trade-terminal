# Cycle Translation
Spot Cycle Tops. Understand Market Strength. Anticipate the Next Move.
Cycle Translation helps you understand how each market cycle unfolded — giving powerful insights into trend direction, exhaustion, and reversal zones. Whether you’re swing trading or managing long-term positions, knowing whether a cycle topped early, mid, or late can drastically sharpen your decision-making.
This feature breaks cycles into three clear types:
* 🟥 Left-Translated Cycles (LTR) – Bearish bias, cycle tops early
* 🟧 Mid-Translated Cycles (MTR) – Neutral balance, tops mid-cycle
* 🟩 Right-Translated Cycles (RTR) – Bullish bias, cycle tops late
***
#### ⚙️ Cycle Translation Settings Box
Full Customisation, Maximum Clarity
* Toggle Display: Turn cycle tops on/off to focus on key reversals
* Icon Colour Coding:
* 🔴 Left-Translated = Early top (potential weakness ahead)
* 🟠 Mid-Translated = Balanced cycle
* 🟢 Right-Translated = Late top (strong cycle momentum)
* Size Adjustments: Scale icons for optimal visibility on any chart style
