# SMA
Smooth Your View. Sharpen Your Strategy.
The Simple Moving Average (SMA) is one of the most reliable tools in a trader’s arsenal. In the CF Cycle Trading Indicator, it adds clarity to market direction by filtering out noise and helping you track trend strength with ease.
***
#### 🔍 Why It Matters for Cycle Trading
When paired with cycle lows and confluence signals, the SMA becomes more than a line — it becomes a guide for trend confirmation, momentum assessment, and entry/exit precision. Whether you’re swing trading or position building, the SMA helps identify where you are in the broader market rhythm.

#### 🛠️ SMA Settings Box
Here’s what you can customise:
* ✅ SWITCH ON/OFF
Turn the SMA line on or off depending on your charting needs.
* \#️⃣ LENGTH
Define how many candles the SMA averages. A longer period = smoother trend line.
* 📊 SOURCE
Choose which data point to base the SMA on — Close, Open, High, Low, etc.
* ↔️ OFFSET
Shift the SMA line forward or backward for clearer visual separation or strategy alignment.
* 📏 WIDTH
Change line thickness to make it bolder or more subtle.
* 🎨 COLOUR
Match your chart aesthetic or create contrast for better visibility.
***
Whether you’re using the SMA as a bias filter or a secondary confirmation layer, it’s built to adapt to your strategy — with no guesswork. Just plug it in, fine-tune, and trade with more confidence.
