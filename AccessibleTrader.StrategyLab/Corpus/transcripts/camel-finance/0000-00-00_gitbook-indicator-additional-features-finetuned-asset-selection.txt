# Finetuned Asset Selection
Precision-Crafted Cycle Counts for Top Assets
The Finetuned Asset Selection feature gives you access to pre-optimised cycle templates tailored to popular assets like BTC, SPX, and XAU/USD — making cycle detection more accurate right out of the box.
***
#### 📊 Why It Matters
Each asset moves with its own rhythm. These curated templates are built using in-depth backtesting and refinement, designed to match each asset’s specific market behaviour. When applied, they enhance the reliability of Daily, Weekly, and Yearly Cycle Low detection.
***
#### 🔁 How It Works
* Select a finetuned template from the dropdown list
* The indicator applies that cycle count logic to your current chart
* Great for analysing assets that haven’t been individually optimised — just borrow logic from one that has similar structure
For example:
Looking at a new altcoin? Try applying the BTC or ETH template for better cycle alignment.
***
#### 🧠 Smart Flexibility
* Not asset-locked: You can apply any optimised template to any chart
* Fast testing: Swap between count presets without needing to manually tweak all settings
* Ideal for forward testing: Especially useful when your asset has limited data or irregular structure
***
#### 🛠️ Refine Even Further
Once you’ve selected a template, go deeper with:
👉 [Cycle Count Adjustment Settings](/camel-finance-features-deep-dive/cf-cycle-trading-indicator/additional-features/asset-count-adjustment.md)
This gives you full control to tweak and customise the count logic further, ensuring the indicator aligns with your unique view of the chart.
