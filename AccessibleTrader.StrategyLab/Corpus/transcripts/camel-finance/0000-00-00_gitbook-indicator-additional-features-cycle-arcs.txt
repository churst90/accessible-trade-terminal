# Cycle Arcs
Visualise Market Rhythm with Precision
Cycle Arcs bring time-based clarity to your charts by visually mapping the expected rhythm and flow of market cycles. These arcs help you spot symmetry, timing windows, and potential shifts — at a glance.
***
#### 🛠️ Customise to Fit Your Style
Inside the Cycle Arcs Settings Box, you’ll find:
* ✅ Switch On/Off – Easily toggle the arcs on or off to reduce clutter when not needed.
* ↕️ UP/DOWN – Shift arc position vertically for better alignment with price.
* 🎯 HEIGHT – Control the arc’s curve depth — taller arcs = more visual impact.
* 📏 WIDTH – Adjust how wide the arc stretches across candles.
* 👁️ VISIBILITY – Make arcs bold or faint with an easy opacity slider.
* 🎨 COLOUR – Match your preferred chart theme with full colour control.
***
#### 🧠 Why Use Cycle Arcs?
These arcs aren’t just for aesthetics — they give traders an edge by:
* Revealing cycle rhythm and timing at a glance
* Highlighting symmetry in price action
* Helping you anticipate when the next key move may occur
* Supporting entry/exit decisions when used with confirmation signals
