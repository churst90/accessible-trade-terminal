# CYCLE LOW ALERTS
Never miss a cycle low again.
Camel Finance’s custom Cycle Low Alert System gives you instant notifications the moment a Daily (🟢 DCL) or Weekly Cycle Low (🔵 WCL) is detected by the CF Cycle Trading Indicator. Whether you’re a swing trader watching for reversal zones or an investor managing multi-asset exposure, these alerts ensure you stay ahead of trend shifts in real time.
✅ Key Benefits:
• Precision-Timed Entries: Get alerted to powerful turning points backed by cycle theory—not noise.
• Fully Customisable Delivery: Receive alerts via TradingView push, email, on-screen, webhook, or sound.
• Watchlist Automation: Paid TradingView users can apply alerts across the entire CF Indicator Asset List.
• Built-In Visual Cues: Toggle alerts on/off for WCL and DCL directly from the indicator interface.
Set it once. Let the system watch the charts for you.
Trade less. Miss less. Catch more. 🔁
