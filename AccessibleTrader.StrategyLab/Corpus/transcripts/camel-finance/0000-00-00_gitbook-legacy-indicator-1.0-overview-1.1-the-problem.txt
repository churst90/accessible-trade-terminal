# THE PROBLEM
Cycle trading is a powerful strategy that relies heavily on identifying and timing market cycles, particularly cycle lows and tops.
📈 However, this process can be incredibly time-consuming and complex, especially for traders who are new to the concept or those managing multiple assets simultaneously.
👨🏻‍💻 Traditional cycle traders often spend countless hours manually counting cycles, analysing past data, and pinpointing lows and tops to enhance the accuracy of their trading decisions.
🔍 This intricate process demands a deep understanding of timing windows, cycle translations, and the overall market structure.
😵‍💫 For many, especially beginners, this can be overwhelming and creates a significant barrier to entry.
