# MID CYCLE POINTS:
The CF Cycle Trading Indicator includes a feature that pinpoints the **Mid-Cycle Lines**—the midpoint between each identified cycle low. It's important to understand that these lines do not detect actual price action or market dips but rather serve as a geometric marker indicating the center of each completed cycle.
📍 **Purpose of Mid-Cycle Lines:** The primary function of these mid-cycle lines is to provide traders with a reference point for the center of a cycle. This can be particularly useful for understanding the overall symmetry and timing within a market cycle. However, these lines are not predictive of price movements or specific market behavior; they simply mark the halfway point between two cycle lows.
🎯 **Application in Trading:** While the mid-cycle line itself does not indicate a change in price direction, it can still offer valuable context for traders. By knowing where the midpoint of a cycle lies, traders can better anticipate the timing of the next cycle low or high, helping them to align their strategies more effectively with the market's rhythm.
