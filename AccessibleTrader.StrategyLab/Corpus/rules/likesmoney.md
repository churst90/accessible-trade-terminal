## Method in its own terms

Every asset has a **daily cycle** (counted in days from the last daily cycle low, DCL) nested inside an **intermediate cycle** (counted in weeks). Each cycle has a **timing band**: the stretch of the count where its low is due. Late in the daily cycle's band, he looks for the lowest point after the cycle's peak. He then waits for a **swing low** that confirms it, and a **close back above the 10-day MA** confirms that a new daily cycle has begun. **Translation** tells him which kind of cycle is unfolding. A right-translated daily cycle (a late peak) trends up "in a stair step fashion". An intermediate cycle decline needs a **left-translated, failed** daily cycle. Trend state comes from a **daily cycle band**: an asset stays in a daily uptrend "until they close below the lower daily cycle band". So a swing low that forms above the lower band is a dip to buy (a "cycle band buy signal" once it closes above the 10-day MA). Supporting evidence near a DCL: a break of the daily cycle trend line, a TSI bearish zero-line cross, a weekly swing, a bullish RSI divergence, and support at the 50-day MA. The mirror signal for a decline is a swing high plus a close below the 10-day MA.

## Context / higher timeframe / regime

- **The intermediate cycle sets what a daily cycle low can be.**
  - Week 18 of the intermediate cycle = "entered their timing band to seek out an intermediate cycle low".
  - The current daily cycle is right translated, so this DCL cannot be the intermediate low. "stocks will need one more daily cycle in order to complete the intermediate cycle decline".
  - He watches for the next daily cycle "to form as a left translated, failed daily cycle".
  - [A Low is Nigh, 2017-03-10](https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/cycle-trading-a-low-is-nigh-201703100509)
- **The left-translation requirement.** "A left translated daily cycle is required for an intermediate cycle decline". [Timing band, 2021-02-16](https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/cycle-trading-timing-band-202102162346)
- **Right-translated daily cycles are bullish in shape.** "Right translated daily cycles trend higher in a stair step fashion". [Right Translation, 2017-10-19](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-right-translation-201710192206)
- **Daily trend state = the lower daily cycle band.** "once an asset is in a daily uptrend, they will remain so until they close below the lower daily cycle band". The band's construction is never given. [Right Translation, 2017-10-19](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-right-translation-201710192206)
- **RSI tells an intermediate decline from a continuation.**
  - "If this was the start of an intermediate cycle decline, then RSI would start to embed in oversold".
  - "The bullish RSI divergence signals a continuation of the intermediate cycle advance". This "aligns with stocks being in a daily uptrend".
  - [Daily cycle low, 2021-02-01](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-daily-cycle-low-202102012343)
- **Change of character.** Russell and Transports corrected while the S&P only formed a swing high. "If there has been a change in character, then we may not see much in the way of a correction for the S&P". This is not defined further. [Right Translation, 2017-10-19](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-right-translation-201710192206)

## Location (levels, zones, where a setup counts)

- **Location is in time first: the timing band.**
  - Day 46 after a day-40 peak = "deep in their timing band to print a daily cycle low" [A Low is Nigh, 2017-03-10](https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/cycle-trading-a-low-is-nigh-201703100509).
  - Day 61 = "late in their timing band for a daily cycle low" [Daily cycle low, 2021-02-01](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-daily-cycle-low-202102012343).
  - XLE after a day-27 peak, lowest point on the column's Thursday = "deep in its timing band for a DCL" (no day number given) [Right Translation, 2017-10-19](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-right-translation-201710192206).
  - **The band's start and end in days are never stated.**
- **The candidate low is "the lowest point following the day N peak"**: the lowest low after the cycle's peak, while in the band. [A Low is Nigh, 2017-03-10](https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/cycle-trading-a-low-is-nigh-201703100509), [Right Translation, 2017-10-19](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-right-translation-201710192206)
- **Above the lower daily cycle band.** A swing low "above the lower daily cycle band" keeps the daily uptrend and makes the dip a buy. [Right Translation, 2017-10-19](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-right-translation-201710192206), [Daily cycle low, 2021-02-01](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-daily-cycle-low-202102012343)
- **Moving-average support.** "Stocks formed a swing low on Monday off of support from the 50 day MA". [Daily cycle low, 2021-02-01](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-daily-cycle-low-202102012343)

## Entry triggers

- **Swing low.**
  - After a bullish reversal day at the low, "A break above 2369.08 forms a swing low and has good odds to mark the daily cycle low". The level is evidently a recent high above the low; the text does not say which bar's high it is.
  - "Thursday's bullish reversal has eased the parameters for forming a swing low".
  - [A Low is Nigh, 2017-03-10](https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/cycle-trading-a-low-is-nigh-201703100509)
- **Swing low plus close above the 10-day MA confirms the new daily cycle.** "At this point a swing low and close back above the 10 day MA will confirm a new daily cycle". [Right Translation, 2017-10-19](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-right-translation-201710192206)
- **Cycle band buy signal.** The swing low formed above the lower daily cycle band. "If stocks deliver bullish follow through and closed above the 10 day MA that will trigger a cycle band buy signal". [Daily cycle low, 2021-02-01](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-daily-cycle-low-202102012343)
- **Buy the dip in a daily uptrend.** "if a swing low forms above the lower daily cycle band the correction strategy is to buy the dip". [Right Translation, 2017-10-19](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-right-translation-201710192206)
- **Bearish mirror (decline signal).** "We will watch for a swing high and close below the 10 day MA as a signal for the daily & intermediate cycle decline". [Timing band, 2021-02-16](https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/cycle-trading-timing-band-202102162346)

## Confirmations (things that raise conviction)

- **Daily cycle trend-line break.** "Normally we would like to see break below the daily cycle trend line before looking for a daily cycle low". He waived it for an "extremely right translated" cycle whose decline would not reach the line. [A Low is Nigh, 2017-03-10](https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/cycle-trading-a-low-is-nigh-201703100509)
- **Signals "normally present as stocks decline into a daily cycle low".** The timing band ("one of the criteria"), "the TSI deliver a bearish zero line crossover", and "the formation a weekly swing". The weekly swing is presumably a weekly swing low; the text does not say. [A Low is Nigh, 2017-03-10](https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/cycle-trading-a-low-is-nigh-201703100509)
- **Bullish RSI divergence at the low** (price lower, RSI higher). Read as the intermediate advance continuing. [Daily cycle low, 2021-02-01](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-daily-cycle-low-202102012343)
- **A late count.** A swing low late in the band "has good odds of marking the DCL". [Daily cycle low, 2021-02-01](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-daily-cycle-low-202102012343)
- **Agreement across related indices** is noted (S&P, Russell, Transports all right translated, all late in their bands). It is not used as an explicit rule. [Right Translation, 2017-10-19](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-right-translation-201710192206)

## Vetoes (things that cancel a trade whatever else says)

- **A close below the lower daily cycle band ends the daily uptrend.** This is implied by "remain so until they close below the lower daily cycle band", so the buy-the-dip logic stops there. [Right Translation, 2017-10-19](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-right-translation-201710192206)
- **RSI embedding in oversold** marks the start of an intermediate decline, not a dip to buy. Implied by the RSI rule above; no length or threshold is given. [Daily cycle low, 2021-02-01](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-daily-cycle-low-202102012343)
- **Do not expect the intermediate low from a right-translated daily cycle.** The intermediate decline needs a left-translated, failed daily cycle. [A Low is Nigh, 2017-03-10](https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/cycle-trading-a-low-is-nigh-201703100509), [Timing band, 2021-02-16](https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/cycle-trading-timing-band-202102162346)

## Calibration numbers and falsifiable side-claims

- **A day-27 peak "assures us of a right translated daily cycle formation"** (XLE). This implies he expects XLE's daily cycle to be shorter than about 54 days; the cycle length itself is not stated. [Right Translation, 2017-10-19](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-right-translation-201710192206)
- **TSI bearish zero-line cross and a weekly swing are "normally present"** as stocks decline into a DCL. Testable: the fraction of confirmed DCLs preceded by a TSI zero-line cross down within the same daily cycle. [A Low is Nigh, 2017-03-10](https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/cycle-trading-a-low-is-nigh-201703100509)
- **Right-translated daily cycles trend higher in a stair step** (each DCL above the last). [Right Translation, 2017-10-19](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-right-translation-201710192206)
- **Once in a daily uptrend, the asset remains in it until a close below the lower daily cycle band.** This is a definition, not a forecast, unless the band is defined independently. [Right Translation, 2017-10-19](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-right-translation-201710192206)
- **A bullish RSI divergence at a DCL means the intermediate advance continues.** Embedded oversold RSI means an intermediate decline. [Daily cycle low, 2021-02-01](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-daily-cycle-low-202102012343)
- **An intermediate decline requires a left-translated daily cycle.** [Timing band, 2021-02-16](https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/cycle-trading-timing-band-202102162346)

## Candidate rules NOT in the pre-registered setup grader

"Cycles as a low detector" and "translation as direction" are dead in this repo. Candidates 1 and 2 use the day count only as a filter on a price trigger. Candidate 5 is a narrower conditional claim than "translation as direction" (left translation is *required for* an intermediate decline, not used to predict one). Pre-register it as such or drop it.

1. **DCL confirmation buy.** Let P be the highest high since the last confirmed DCL, and L the lowest low after P. Buy on the first close where all of these hold:
   - (a) the bar count since the last DCL is ≥ a band start B. B is not stated: pre-register B as 0.8× the median of the last 4 DCL-to-DCL spans, or test fixed values;
   - (b) close > the high of the bar that made L (the swing-low break);
   - (c) close > SMA(10).

   The confirmed bar becomes the new DCL anchor. Compare with the same trigger without the day-count filter, and with exposure-matched random entries. Uses the terminal's SMA and cycles tooling. [A Low is Nigh, 2017-03-10](https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/cycle-trading-a-low-is-nigh-201703100509), [Right Translation, 2017-10-19](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-right-translation-201710192206)
2. **Cycle band buy signal.** As candidate 1, plus the swing low L must hold above a lower daily cycle band. The band is undefined; pre-register a proxy and say so, e.g. the lowest low of the prior DCL, or SMA(10) − k·ATR. The trend-state exit is the first close below that band. This candidate is the method's core but **needs a definition we do not have**. [Right Translation, 2017-10-19](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-right-translation-201710192206), [Daily cycle low, 2021-02-01](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-daily-cycle-low-202102012343)
3. **Swing-high plus 10-day-MA-break exit or short.** After a daily-cycle peak: a confirmed swing high (a bar whose high is above the highs of the bars either side; the width is not stated, pre-register 1 bar each side) followed by a close < SMA(10). Test it as an exit for candidate 1's longs, against the grader's exits. Test it also as a short trigger when the intermediate count is ≥ week 21. [Timing band, 2021-02-16](https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/cycle-trading-timing-band-202102162346)
4. **RSI divergence versus embedding at the low.** At a candidate DCL, classify:
   - (i) bullish divergence: price low below the previous swing low while RSI(14) is above its value at that low;
   - (ii) embedded: RSI(14) < 30 for ≥ 3 of the last 5 bars.

   The RSI length, the 30 level and the 3-of-5 rule are pre-registered guesses, not stated. Test whether (i) is followed by a higher next daily cycle and (ii) by a lower one. Uses the terminal's RSI. [Daily cycle low, 2021-02-01](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-daily-cycle-low-202102012343)
5. **Intermediate lows only after a left-translated daily cycle (conditional).** For each confirmed intermediate low (the lowest DCL of an intermediate cycle), check whether the daily cycle ending at it peaked before its midpoint. Measure the rate against the base rate of left translation across all daily cycles. Descriptive. It needs an intermediate-cycle labelling rule, which the columns do not give. [Timing band, 2021-02-16](https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/cycle-trading-timing-band-202102162346), [A Low is Nigh, 2017-03-10](https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/cycle-trading-a-low-is-nigh-201703100509)
6. **TSI zero-line cross as a pre-DCL condition.** Require a TSI bearish zero-line cross within the current daily cycle (since its peak) before a candidate-1 buy is allowed. Test whether it improves forward returns over candidate 1 alone. TSI lengths are not stated; pre-register (25, 13). The terminal does not list a TSI; it needs adding or computing in the lab. [A Low is Nigh, 2017-03-10](https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/cycle-trading-a-low-is-nigh-201703100509)
7. **50-day MA support swing low.** A candidate-1 swing low whose low L is within x% of SMA(50) and closes above it. Compare against swing lows away from the 50-day. Uses the terminal's SMA. [Daily cycle low, 2021-02-01](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/cycle-trading-daily-cycle-low-202102012343)
