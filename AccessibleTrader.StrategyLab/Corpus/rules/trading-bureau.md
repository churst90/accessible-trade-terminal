## Method in its own terms

Jordan's MC B trades need **three Market Cipher confluences**, all in the trade's direction: (1) the **dot** printed beyond his ±45 line; (2) the **VWAP crossing** zero; (3) **money flow pointing the same way**, read as an "arrow" of its general trend, not its colour. He adds outside confluences (candle pattern, volume-profile position, S/R, Fibonacci, moving averages) until he has **at least five**. His "trigger wave" is not an anchor→trigger pair. It is **a single dot on a "shark fin" wave around the 45 level**. Money flow is "the most important part". If the dot and VWAP agree but money flow points the other way, he **waits for money flow to turn** before entering, or skips the trade. The stop goes beyond the swing with a buffer. He takes most of the position off at 1:1, moves the stop to breakeven and aims for 1:3, leaving a runner.

## Anchor wave, trigger wave, money flow and confirmation (exact)

- **Anchor wave.** Nothing stated. He never uses the term.
- **Trigger wave (his definition).** "It is simply a green or red dot on our wave trend at a shark fin pattern around about the 45 level" ([Trigger Waves, 2025-11-26, 00:40](https://youtu.be/q4UdlkQScmg?t=40)). For longs the dot must be "around the 45 level or below it" ([Trigger Waves, 2025-11-26, 05:20](https://youtu.be/q4UdlkQScmg?t=320)). That means at or below −45 (his "45 level" is used for both sides). He says "a nice shock [shark] fin pattern that you always want to look out for" ([Trigger Waves, 2025-11-26, 02:44](https://youtu.be/q4UdlkQScmg?t=164)). **The shark fin shape is never defined** (no slope or bar count).
- **Depth threshold (exact).** He sets MC B "wave trend overbought level two" = 45 and "oversold level two" = 45 (meaning −45), with the other overbought/oversold levels at 0, so that a white line marks the entry zone ([Trigger Waves, 2025-11-26, 01:11](https://youtu.be/q4UdlkQScmg?t=71)). "For more conservative traders, you can use the 60 level". The really experienced might go lower ([Trigger Waves, 2025-11-26, 01:42](https://youtu.be/q4UdlkQScmg?t=102)).
- **What a dot is.** "our wave trend crossed its own slow moving average causing a sell dot" ([Trigger Waves, 2025-11-26, 02:12](https://youtu.be/q4UdlkQScmg?t=132)).
- **Time between waves.** Nothing stated.
- **Money flow requirement.**
  - The **direction** of the money flow trend has to match the trade. Colour does not matter. "Try to understand it as an arrow with a directional point" ([Money Flow, 2026-03-03, 03:44](https://youtu.be/90kAAraFOS4?t=224)).
  - Read the "general flow", "cutting out the noise" of spikes, with "your pattern recognition brain" ([Money Flow, 2026-03-03, 01:43](https://youtu.be/90kAAraFOS4?t=103)).
  - The best long in his examples came when money flow was "creating higher lows and trending upwards" ([Money Flow, 2026-03-03, 04:14](https://youtu.be/90kAAraFOS4?t=254)).
  - A cross below zero means money flowing out, and a cross into green means money flowing in ([Money Flow, 2026-03-03, 02:13](https://youtu.be/90kAAraFOS4?t=133)).
  - No "thick" or "deep green" vocabulary, no number. Same timeframe as the signal.
- **What he WAITS FOR after a green dot prints (exact).** **Money flow turning up.** On the 4H example, "when we got the first two levels of confluence of the green dot printing and the VWAP crossing up, our money flow was still heading in a downwards direction. Once we started to see that money flow had turned around to an upwards sloping direction, that is when we could have got bullish" ([Trigger Waves, 2025-11-26, 05:52](https://youtu.be/q4UdlkQScmg?t=352)). The bearish version is the 10 Oct crash: a bearish divergence and a VWAP cross down printed while money flow pointed up, "a sign to hold your horses". The short came "when money flow started to tank" ([Money Flow, 2026-03-03, 03:44](https://youtu.be/90kAAraFOS4?t=224); [Money Flow, 2026-03-03, 04:14](https://youtu.be/90kAAraFOS4?t=254)). He also needs five confluences in total before drawing the trade ([Trigger Waves, 2025-11-26, 03:16](https://youtu.be/q4UdlkQScmg?t=196)). No candle-close rule is stated.
- **How a dot is invalidated.** If money flow never turns your way, there is no trade. "the green dot printed below the 45 degree line, our VWAP was crossing up but our money flow was clearly trending in a downwards direction ... we did not take the trade" ([Money Flow, 2026-03-03, 04:45](https://youtu.be/90kAAraFOS4?t=285); [Money Flow, 2026-03-03, 05:15](https://youtu.be/90kAAraFOS4?t=315)).

## Vetoes (things that cancel a trade whatever else says)

- **Money flow trending against the trade** = no trade, even with a dot beyond 45 and a VWAP cross ([Money Flow, 2026-03-03, 04:45](https://youtu.be/90kAAraFOS4?t=285); [Money Flow, 2026-03-03, 05:15](https://youtu.be/90kAAraFOS4?t=315)). The veto is on **slope or direction, regardless of sign**. The grader's veto needs MF < 0 AND falling.
- Second "bad money flow" example: a red dot above 45 and the VWAP crossed, "but at that point, our money flow was pointing down and it gave us a very small move" ([Money Flow, 2026-03-03, 05:15](https://youtu.be/90kAAraFOS4?t=315)). As transcribed this contradicts his own rule, since money flow pointing down would agree with a short. It is probably a slip for "up". Do not use it.

## Candidate rules NOT in the pre-registered setup grader

1. **The ±45 dot threshold.** A buy dot = WT1 crosses up through WT2 with the wave at or below −45 (the grader uses WT2 ≤ −60). Test −45 versus −60 inside the same grader. Computable. Terminal: Cipher B levels are configurable.
2. **Wait for money flow to turn up after the dot (timing rule).** After a buy dot at bar d, enter at the first bar t ≥ d (within N bars, N not stated) where MF(t) > MF(t−1), preferably also making a higher low. If MF(t) ≤ MF(t−1) for all t up to d+N, there is no trade. This differs from the grader's confirmation ("MF rising two bars in a row" checked *at* the dot): here entry is **delayed** until the turn. Computable.
3. **Money flow slope veto regardless of sign.** No long while MF is falling, even if MF > 0. Grader veto requires MF < 0 AND falling. Computable.
4. **The VWAP zero cross as a separate confluence.** In MC B the dots and the VWAP crossing are listed as two confluences. In VuManChu's open code the yellow "VWAP" is WT1 − WT2 (our reading of that script, not stated here), so its zero cross is the same event as the dot. If the terminal's VWAP is built that way, this confluence adds nothing. Check before counting it.
5. **1:1 partial + breakeven + 1:3 final with a runner.** Fixed-R exits are on the repo's dead list, so test this only to reproduce his full system.
