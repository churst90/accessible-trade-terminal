Method in plain words: nested cycles (daily inside weekly inside 4-year, "Russian nesting dolls"). He waits for a daily cycle low (DCL) to fall inside its timing window, confirms it with a short checklist (swing low, daily close above the 10 SMA, close above a down trendline, DSS Bressert bullish cross, his CF indicator "picking" the low, a big bullish candle), goes long late rather than early, and parks the stop under that low. The live trade is a stop placement, not a price forecast ("Cycles predict time, not price"). If the cycle peaks early (left translation) and then breaks below its starting low (failure), he flips short. A weekly regime gate (HY spreads, 10y-2y, 10y-3m) decides whether breakdowns are taken seriously. A hand-drawn "yellow squiggle" is his projected path for each asset, and each has a named invalidation (or, for oil, none).
