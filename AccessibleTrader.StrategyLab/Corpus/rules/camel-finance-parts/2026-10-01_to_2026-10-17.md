**The method in plain words (as used in October).** He trades nested cycles across assets. Each asset has its own multi-year cycle: Bitcoin 4-year, gold/silver 8-year (the "half cycle" in it is the 4-year low), TLT 7.5-year, DXY 3-year. He also tracks a weekly cycle and a daily cycle in each. He plans with "if-then" conditions. A cycle low is accepted when a pivot forms inside the timing window and is confirmed by a DSS Bressert cross and a trendline break. What happens next decides the bigger picture. If the new daily cycle makes a higher high (right translation), the bigger low is taken as in. If it tops early, makes a lower high and closes below the confirmed low ("left translates and fails"), a lower low is coming at the next degree up. In October he attaches a number to this: **80% either way**. Bitcoin broke his August invalidation level (the "most important level in finance", 83K in August). He then switched his base case from "4-year low still ahead" to "buy a higher-low weekly-cycle pullback". He kept the bear path as a named alternative with stated triggers. He sizes for both outcomes. He takes a small long on the daily cycle low and willingly pays a ~2% stop to keep exposure, saving the heavy buying for the weekly cycle low. Equities are traded as a blow-off top until "alarm signals" fire: trendline breakdowns plus failed daily cycles plus HY spreads blowing out. Macro (disinflation, labour weakness, "tell that to the bond market") is the stated justification for the positions, not a trigger.

## Candidate rules NOT in the pre-registered setup grader

1. **DCL resolution as a low-is-in classifier (BTC).** After a confirmed daily cycle low near a candidate 4-year low, classify the outcome. "Low in" = a close above the prior daily-cycle high before any close below the DCL low. "Low not in" = a lower high, then a close below the DCL low. Score: did a lower low than the candidate low print within the next 26 weeks? He claims 80% either way [It's Happening!, 2026-10-13, 06:18](https://youtu.be/qarjzOUTk8k?t=378). Computable with a fixed swing/cycle detector (the DSS Bressert cross out of oversold as the DCL confirmation), but n is tiny: one or two BTC 4-year lows per cycle. Better framed as a risk-placement test across many assets. Note the overlap with the dead "translation as direction" test. This is specifically *new high before failure* as the classifier, not translation timing.
2. **BTC-proxy lag-and-buy.** After BTC's cycle low (a confirmed weekly pivot), test whether BTC-linked equities (COIN, MSTR, RIOT, MARA, CLSK, BITF, HUT, CIFR, BTBT, IREN, WULF, HIVE) make a lower low within N weeks, and whether buying that later low beats buying BTC at its low over 12–24 months, with risk capped at 2% per trade [How I outperformed Bitcoin last cycle, 2026-10-14, 03:40](https://youtu.be/52ZlNcndcSE?t=220), [How I outperformed Bitcoin last cycle, 2026-10-14, 08:27](https://youtu.be/52ZlNcndcSE?t=507). Computable with daily equity data. The 2018–19 and 2022–23 cycles give about 12 names × 2 episodes. Control: buy the same names at BTC's low, and at random dates.
3. **Failed weekly cycle means the next low is lower.** Weekly cycle = the span between confirmed weekly pivots (fixed detector). Failed = a weekly close below the cycle's starting low. Claim: the next weekly-cycle low prints below the failed cycle's low [How I outperformed Bitcoin last cycle, 2026-10-14, 02:37](https://youtu.be/52ZlNcndcSE?t=157). Computable. It is close to the dead translation work, so test only this "next low lower" conditional, against surrogates.
4. **S&P Q4 seasonality conditional.** If S&P YTD return at the 30 Sep close is between +10% and +20%, Q4 return > 0 in 85% of years, averaging +5.3% [A Bullish Q4 is Loading !!, 2026-10-02, 03:47](https://youtu.be/MG8WI9O0Eyk?t=227). Computable from S&P history. The control is the unconditional Q4 up-rate and mean.
5. **Credit-spread timed exit.** Go flat by mid-week if (a) the HY OAS (FRED BAMLH0A0HYM2) closes above its trailing range high (e.g. the 26-week high) **and** (b) the S&P closes lower on each day of the prior week (or the week is down more than X%). Otherwise hold. A poke above the range that closes back inside within a week is ignored [The Next Leg Higher has Started!, 2026-10-03, 13:57](https://youtu.be/IYfTEZtA2f8?t=837), [This Changes Things, 2026-10-17, 14:20](https://youtu.be/l4d4MOUG1Gg?t=860). Computable. Benchmark against `close > SMA(200)` as in the existing regime-gate queue item.
6. **Yields-then-stocks lag.** When the 10y yield makes a confirmed weekly-cycle top, stocks top 3–4 months later, and commodities after that [A Bullish Q4 is Loading !!, 2026-10-02, 02:41](https://youtu.be/MG8WI9O0Eyk?t=161). Computable on the 10y (FRED DGS10), S&P and a commodity index with a fixed pivot detector. Only a handful of episodes (2000, 2007, …), so expect Inconclusive.
7. **Equity half-cycle timing (risk-placement version).** On S&P/US100 with daily cycles counted in trading days, the half-cycle low falls at day 20 (±2–5), the daily cycle low at day 40 ±4, and a right-translated top at day 30–35 [It's Happening!, 2026-10-13, 01:01](https://youtu.be/qarjzOUTk8k?t=61), [This Changes Things, 2026-10-17, 16:28](https://youtu.be/l4d4MOUG1Gg?t=988). Test as stop placement: is a stop under a day-18–24 swing low hit less often than an equidistant stop placed at a random day? Cycles as *detectors* are dead; this is the narrower risk-placement claim.
8. **Gold/metals low trio.** Buy when (a) a swing low is confirmed, (b) DSS Bressert (10,9,5) crosses up from below 20, and (c) the close is above a trendline [It's Happening!, 2026-10-13, 02:05](https://youtu.be/qarjzOUTk8k?t=125). (a) and (b) are computable with terminal indicators. (c) is a hand-drawn line; substitute "close above the prior lower swing high". This overlaps the August checklist. The new parts are the gold-specific window (week 22–28 of the weekly cycle) and the order: underlying first, miners after the halfway point of the rally [Here's my Trading Plan for the Week, 2026-10-05, 02:12](https://youtu.be/fMQyp-wqH8Y?t=132), [It's Happening!, 2026-10-13, 02:36](https://youtu.be/qarjzOUTk8k?t=156).
