# Camel Finance: extracted rules (PART 2 of the source: 2026-08-11 to 2026-08-31)

Source slug `camel-finance`; channel Camel Finance (public YouTube daily videos). This part covers **17 transcripts**, 2026-08-11 through 2026-08-31 inclusive (no files for 16, 23, 29, 30 Aug). Material: daily live market updates, two weekend "deep dives" (15 and 22 Aug), one pre-recorded Q&A (18 Aug, recorded Friday 14 Aug per its own [00:34]) and one teaching video (28 Aug). Access: public. Live entries and stops are posted to "Level 3" members (paid) and are generally NOT spoken, so most positions below have no entry price on air. Transcripts are Whisper large-v3-turbo; "Bressa", "Bresset", "breast", "brecet" = DSS Bressert; "Siljay/Steeljay" = SILJ; "ball market/ball flag" = bull market/bull flag; "Campbell" = Camel.

**Method in plain words.** Nested time cycles (daily inside weekly inside yearly/4-year; "Russian nesting dolls") give a *timing window* for the next low. Inside the window he waits for a swing low to be *confirmed* on that cycle's own timeframe (DSS Bressert bullish cross, close back above the 10 SMA, trendline break, a big green thrust candle, his CF indicator marking the low, optionally a Parabolic SAR flip). He then buys with a stop a little below the cycle low, sized so the stop costs about 2% of the account, and adds more at each later higher low. A pre-drawn "yellow squiggle" path is the base case. Each thesis has a written-down invalidation, most importantly **a higher-high *close* above the prior swing high** (BTC ~83K in August). Before it he will not call a bull market; after it, "it is malpractice to be bearish". He prefers buying a retest/higher low to buying the low itself, because the stop is tighter and so the position can be bigger for the same account risk. A separate macro "alarm" gate (high-yield spreads, yield curves) decides whether dips are bought at all.
