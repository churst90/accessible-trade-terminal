**The method in this window, in plain words.** Same nested-cycle framework (daily inside weekly inside 4-year), traded by waiting for a cycle low in its timing window, confirming it (his indicator labels the low, a DSS Bressert bullish cross, a trendline break) and placing the risk line under it. The window's big event: **BTC broke his pre-registered 83K line on 22 Sep and he flipped his base case from "4-year low still to come, Dec 2026 to Feb 2027" to "early 4-year low already in (16 Aug), buy the higher-low pullback into the next weekly cycle low at year end"**, keeping the old path as Plan B, triggered by a left-translated failed daily cycle. In the same window gold went the other way: he bought the "higher low" on 18 Sep, the daily cycle failed on 28 Sep, and he moved his gold squiggle about two months to the right on 29 Sep. His plan from 23 Sep onward is to buy the year-end weekly cycle low in BTC, gold, miners and MSTR "whether higher or lower", which makes the buy itself impossible to falsify. Only the invalidation lines he hangs off it can be checked. The 17 Sep video is the clearest statement anywhere in the corpus of his two sizing systems (short-term: fixed 2% risk and hard stops; long-term: position size assuming the asset goes to zero, added in 5% steps at confirmed cycle lows).
