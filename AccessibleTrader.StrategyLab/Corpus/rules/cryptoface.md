Vocabulary as he uses it (Market Cipher B unless noted): "blue waves" / "momentum waves" / "momentum algorithm" = the WaveTrend pair (dark blue wave = WT1 area, "white" or "light blue" = the faster line); "the white cuts into the blue", "pinch off", "clip", "nipped off" = the WaveTrend cross; "money flow" / "transaction flow" / "red and green waves" = the money flow area; "VWAP" / "V oscillation" / "yellow waves" (setting name "trend three") = the yellow VWAP area; "RSIs" = the single colour-coded smoothed RSI line; "green dot" / "big boy green dot" = Cipher B buy dot; "snake eyes" = two green dots, "Cerberus" = three; "bullets" = independent confirmations.

## Method in its own terms

"Identify your environment" first: start on the largest timeframes and work down, reading the money flow (thick green = money coming in, long the dips; red = money leaving, short the peaks) and whether the larger timeframes "have your back". Then take a "basic strategy" entry on a lower timeframe only when three or four "bullets" line up in the same direction: a money flow wave in the red that has visibly curved back up (enter about three-quarters of the way through the drawn-out wave), a deep "anchor" momentum wave followed by one or more smaller "trigger" waves whose light line cuts into the dark blue and holds for a candle or two, RSIs green (oversold) and turning up, VWAP turning up, and green dots as the "handle of the gun" that start the search rather than end it. A yellow X on the dot candle overrides the dot. Exits are "enter in the red, exit in the green": take profit in pieces when the money flow is thick in the green and starts to curve, or when the light momentum line cuts back the other way. He estimates the holding time by drawing the expected money-flow wave and measuring it, scales in and out in 2-4 pieces, keeps a separate account per timeframe, never averages down a loser, and (2025-2026) trades a bear regime as a "roach pig": out as soon as in profit, bank 30% of gains, and treat green dots as temporary bottoms.
