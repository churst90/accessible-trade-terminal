## Method in its own terms

"Confluence" as a checklist. In MC B, read the **shape of money flow** (smooth outward curve = continuation, inward curve = weakening) rather than its colour. Then read whether the **RSI and stochastic RSI** agree and sit close together. Then read the **momentum waves**: dots on crosses, larger green dots at potential bottoms, and consecutive higher lows for a bullish move ("anchor wave" then "trigger wave"). DBSI adds a timeframe-adaptive moving average (red/green) and a per-candle bull versus bear score. A bullish score that overtakes the bearish one, while VWAP, money flow, momentum and RSI agree, is a confluence point. Nothing is to be used alone, and no stop, target or timeframe rule is given.

## Anchor wave, trigger wave, money flow and confirmation (exact)

- **Anchor and trigger wave (all that is said).** "You may also hear these described using the terms anchor wave and trigger wave, where a larger initial momentum wave is followed by a smaller wave that can provide additional confirmation" ([Market Cipher B tutorial, 2026-10-09, 02:33](https://youtu.be/GbT7iCVdaq8?t=153)). For a bullish move, "look for progressively higher lows" between consecutive waves ([Market Cipher B tutorial, 2026-10-09, 02:03](https://youtu.be/GbT7iCVdaq8?t=123); [Market Cipher B tutorial, 2026-10-09, 02:33](https://youtu.be/GbT7iCVdaq8?t=153)). **No depth, no line value, no size ratio and no gap in bars.**
- **Dots.** A momentum wave crossing upward "can produce a green dot confirmation". "Larger green dots can also highlight potential market bottoms, although they should not be treated as guarantees" ([Market Cipher B tutorial, 2026-10-09, 02:03](https://youtu.be/GbT7iCVdaq8?t=123)). The vendor's tagline for the green dot is "market bottoms" ([Market Cipher B tutorial, 2026-10-09, 00:32](https://youtu.be/GbT7iCVdaq8?t=32)).
- **Money flow (exact as stated).** "Rather than focusing on its color, pay attention to the shape and direction of the wave. ## DBSI rules (exact)

- **Two tools** ([Market Cipher DBSI tutorial, 2026-10-09, 00:00](https://youtu.be/nP3UGFne8N8?t=0); [Market Cipher DBSI tutorial, 2026-10-09, 00:30](https://youtu.be/nP3UGFne8N8?t=30)):
  - a **dynamic moving average line** "which adjusts to the timeframe you're viewing". It is red when price is below the average and green above. A red→green transition "can indicate potential bullish movement", but "should not be used by itself".
  - a **per-candle numerical score**, bears versus bulls.
- **Score scale (exact).** "The score ranges from -5 at the weakest to 31 at the strongest". Example: bears 17 and bulls −2 means "bearish pressure is clearly dominant" ([Market Cipher DBSI tutorial, 2026-10-09, 01:00](https://youtu.be/nP3UGFne8N8?t=60)). The 2021 reviewers said −5 to 28 (see `dbsi-reviewers.md`). The upper bound may have changed between versions.
- **What a signal is.** "When bullish scores begin strengthening and overtake bearish scores, it can indicate that bulls are beginning to take control" (mirror for bears) ([Market Cipher DBSI tutorial, 2026-10-09, 01:00](https://youtu.be/nP3UGFne8N8?t=60); [Market Cipher DBSI tutorial, 2026-10-09, 01:30](https://youtu.be/nP3UGFne8N8?t=90)). No numeric threshold (no "14") is given here.
- **Indecision.** "When the bull and bear scores are close together, the market may be showing less conviction" ([Market Cipher DBSI tutorial, 2026-10-09, 01:30](https://youtu.be/nP3UGFne8N8?t=90)).
- **What to wait for.** Agreement from the MC B parts, "VWAP, momentum, money flow, and RSI" ([Market Cipher DBSI tutorial, 2026-10-09, 02:00](https://youtu.be/nP3UGFne8N8?t=120)). In the training example, "a bullish score of 25 appeared after a bearish run". At the same time the VWAP crossed above zero, money flow moved up, and momentum and RSI turned bullish ([Market Cipher DBSI tutorial, 2026-10-09, 02:31](https://youtu.be/nP3UGFne8N8?t=151); [Market Cipher DBSI tutorial, 2026-10-09, 03:01](https://youtu.be/nP3UGFne8N8?t=181)).
- "These scores do not guarantee what the next candle will do" ([Market Cipher DBSI tutorial, 2026-10-09, 01:30](https://youtu.be/nP3UGFne8N8?t=90)).

## Candidate rules NOT in the pre-registered setup grader

1. **Money flow curvature.** Classify MF's second difference over the last 3 bars: "outward" (|MF| growing at an increasing rate, i.e. accelerating away from zero) means continuation; "inward" (decelerating) means weakening or reversal. Test whether an inward-curving negative MF at a buy dot improves outcomes over the grader's "MF rising two bars". Computable. Terminal: Cipher B money flow.
2. **RSI/StochRSI tightness.** At a buy dot, |RSI − StochRSI(K)| below a threshold, with both rising. Uses the terminal's RSI/Stochastic as a proxy for MC B's lines. Computable, but a proxy.
3. **DBSI bull score overtaking bear score** (cross of the two bands). Needs the vendor's DBSI (closed). The terminal's DBSI-style rebuild is only a proxy.

## Not usable / vague

- Anchor/trigger: a definition by size only ("larger ... followed by a smaller").
- "Smooth outward curve": needs a smoothing choice.
- Everything is framed as "may", "can indicate", "not guarantees". There are no thresholds apart from the DBSI scale.
