## Method in its own terms

**Crypto Wolf.** Memorise a recurring pattern, the **"triple anchor wave"** ("triple camel"). It is a strong anchor wave, "usually over the 60 line", followed by two more waves, then a trigger wave. On price it is a three-push divergence: price makes a triple top while momentum makes lower highs. Some traders short after the first or second divergence. He waits for the third, because "when you see the three there's usually never a four". The best entry is while the confirming dot is still printing. If missed, wait for the trigger wave as a "second chance", and a lower trigger can be a third. His example is bearish. He uses a "pretty loose stop loss".

## Candidate rules NOT in the pre-registered setup grader

1. **Crypto Wolf's triple-wave divergence (computable).**
   - Find three consecutive WT2 local maxima M1 > M2 > M3, with M1 ≥ +60 (bearish), on bars where price makes highs H1 ≤ H2 ≤ H3 (or within a tolerance).
   - Short on the bearish cross that ends wave 3, or on the next lower-high trigger.
   - Mirror for longs: three WT2 minima m1 < m2 < m3 with m1 ≤ −60 while price makes equal or lower lows; buy the third wave's green dot.
   - Test against single and double divergences: does the third push carry more edge, and how often does a fourth occur?
   - The grader's divergence confirmation is any bullish divergence within 5 bars. It has no wave-count condition. Terminal: Cipher B divergences.
2. **Second-chance trigger.** If the third-wave dot is missed, enter at the next WT cross whose extreme is closer to zero than wave 3's. Computable.
