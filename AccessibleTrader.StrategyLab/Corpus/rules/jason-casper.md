## Method in its own terms

Casper trades Market Cipher B in a top-down, multi-timeframe way, and he insists on levels. First he marks support and resistance "zones": candle-body open/close clusters starting on the daily, Fibonacci golden pocket/0.786, value-area and point-of-control levels, and the 21 EMA. He writes a plan for what he wants to see when price reaches a zone. He does not look at the indicator until price gets there. On the higher timeframe (2H–4H, or the weekly for swings) he wants Market Cipher B to be printing, or about to print, a bottom or top. He calls a deep bottoming wave the anchor wave; it "usually" goes below −60. The next, higher wave in the same direction is the trigger wave. On the lower execution timeframe he then waits for a clear direction and a money flow crossover. His rule of thumb for that timeframe is the higher one divided by 10: 4H→24m, 3H→18m, 2H→12m, 50m→5m. His other pair is 3H with 12m. His strongest signal is a divergence in both the momentum waves AND the money flow at a level. A higher-timeframe hidden divergence combined with a lower-timeframe regular divergence is a high-probability continuation. Stops go beyond the last swing high or low, or the 21 EMA. He targets prior support and resistance, at least 2:1. He takes partial profits, typically 25–50% at the first target, then moves the stop to entry. He exits early when the money flow turns or the higher-timeframe waves curve over before the opposite dot. He risks 0.5–5% of the account per trade, 1% as standard. In 2026 his Bitcoin videos add weekly and monthly Market Cipher B dots and divergences, market structure (higher highs and lows on weekly closes), Fibonacci and volume-profile levels, and a four-year-cycle "365 days after the all-time high" timing idea.

## Anchor wave, trigger wave, money flow and confirmation (exact)

**Summary of how exact the source is.** He gives one number for the anchor: "usually" below the −60 line. He gives one number for a veto: no 4/24 long when the 4H green dot prints above +60. The rest is qualitative and changes from video to video. He never mentions a dotted line. He never says how many bars may separate the anchor and trigger. He never uses the phrase "deep green". "Thick" money flow means a money flow wave of large magnitude. Most important for the round-2 pre-registration: **his "wait after the dot" happens on a LOWER timeframe in almost every strategy.** He takes the higher-timeframe dot or trigger wave, then waits for a lower-timeframe money flow zero-cross or trigger dot. The exceptions are the single-timeframe 1-hour divergence strategy (wait for a second dot that forms a divergence) and the "long the trigger wave, not the anchor wave" rule. Both of those translate to daily bars.

### Anchor wave
- **Depth.** "an anchor wave is a nice deep momentum wave that usually goes down below the negative 60 line". A large green dot on it makes it "more of a powerful signal" but is not required ([4/24 Beginner Strategy, 2024-02-06, 02:02](https://youtu.be/eIJBiUakLN0?t=122)–[4/24 Beginner Strategy, 2024-02-06, 02:33](https://youtu.be/eIJBiUakLN0?t=153)). This is the only numeric depth in the corpus, and it says "usually", not "must".
- **Anchor as "big old deep wave, preferably with a big old dot"** when it is under the oscillator. At the top of the oscillator "you can look at any wave that's high as an anchor wave" ([2 Powerful Scalping Tips, 2021-08-19, 27:10](https://youtu.be/UdsLO5OQ6b8?t=1630)–[2 Powerful Scalping Tips, 2021-08-19, 27:42](https://youtu.be/UdsLO5OQ6b8?t=1662)).
- **Anchor as a structural reference point, not a depth.** In 2021 he defined it as "an established bottom, a point, a reference point from which on both sides we can see direction moving in the opposite way from this wave". It is "less of just a big old wave, it's less of looking for a dot" ([How to Find the Best Trades, 2021-03-18, 24:00](https://youtu.be/Ma2lR62m4nA?t=1440)–[How to Find the Best Trades, 2021-03-18, 24:32](https://youtu.be/Ma2lR62m4nA?t=1472)). He also calls a wave an anchor simply "because it's lower than this one here" ([How to Find the Best Trades, 2021-03-18, 06:43](https://youtu.be/Ma2lR62m4nA?t=403)), so anchor status can be relative.
- **Anchor = attention, not entry.** "The anchor wave is telling you it's time to start looking to enter the trade. It doesn't mean to enter." ([2 Powerful Scalping Tips, 2021-08-19, 27:10](https://youtu.be/UdsLO5OQ6b8?t=1630)).
- **Double anchors.** A viewer said two anchor waves close together on the 4H usually precede a strong uptrend, and he agreed ("I absolutely agree"). His examples were 4H double bottoms followed by run-ups ([How to Find the Best Trades, 2021-03-18, 61:42](https://youtu.be/Ma2lR62m4nA?t=3702)–[How to Find the Best Trades, 2021-03-18, 62:13](https://youtu.be/Ma2lR62m4nA?t=3733)). He gave no count or spacing.
- **Anchor depth on the daily for ETH.** A daily green dot "below the 60" means "we could still have a lot more a lot more upward momentum" before a top ([Finding PERFECT Entries, 2021-04-10, 36:01](https://youtu.be/EwtsVvxt30g?t=2161)). The sign is ambiguous in the audio. The context (a daily buy dot) implies −60.

### Trigger wave
- **Definition 1 (2025): any higher wave after a deep one.** "a trigger wave simply means after you print a really deep wave any higher wave is called a trigger wave" ([Divergence Masterclass, 2025-05-21, 13:45](https://youtu.be/11pOQcHI9SU?t=825)).
- **Definition 2 (2024): a continuation wave whose money flow is also higher.** The momentum wave is higher than the anchor, and "a very powerful thing here is the money flow is the money flow also getting higher as that momentum wave is getting higher". If the money flow is not higher "you could still take a trade but the probabilities are not as good" ([4/24 Beginner Strategy, 2024-02-06, 02:33](https://youtu.be/eIJBiUakLN0?t=153)–[4/24 Beginner Strategy, 2024-02-06, 03:03](https://youtu.be/eIJBiUakLN0?t=183)).
- **Where it prints.** The trigger after a regular bullish divergence is "that next momentum wave that prints below the zero line". He says about 90% of the time it gives "your next big impulse to the upside" ([Divergence Masterclass, 2025-05-21, 13:45](https://youtu.be/11pOQcHI9SU?t=825)–[Divergence Masterclass, 2025-05-21, 14:16](https://youtu.be/11pOQcHI9SU?t=856)). He repeats it: after the divergence, "the next time the oscillator comes down and prints a wave below the zero line that's going to be our next big move" ([Divergence Masterclass, 2025-05-21, 17:30](https://youtu.be/11pOQcHI9SU?t=1050)).
- **A trigger can also be well above zero** in a trend, "even though it's so high, is because we're still getting the direction upward" ([How to Find the Best Trades, 2021-03-18, 24:32](https://youtu.be/Ma2lR62m4nA?t=1472)).
- **How much smaller.** No number. He wants a sequence: anchor, then a higher low wave, preferably "consecutively higher trigger waves, higher and higher, and then you get this money flow crossover" ([Finding PERFECT Entries, 2021-04-10, 06:57](https://youtu.be/EwtsVvxt30g?t=417)). His long template is a green wave down, then a red wave, then "a tight green wave right here and this money flow is clearly crossing over" ([Finding PERFECT Entries, 2021-04-10, 16:11](https://youtu.be/EwtsVvxt30g?t=971)). He mentions a "cluster of triggers" on the 50-minute chart as a live idea he had not tested ([How to Find the Best Trades, 2021-03-18, 52:21](https://youtu.be/Ma2lR62m4nA?t=3141)–[How to Find the Best Trades, 2021-03-18, 54:25](https://youtu.be/Ma2lR62m4nA?t=3265)).
- **Trade the trigger, not the anchor (counter-trend).** In a 4H downtrend, "don't long the anchor wave. Long the trigger wave". After the bottom, hope that price comes up, prints a red dot, then "a higher green dot as we come down to a level of support" ([2 Powerful Scalping Tips, 2021-08-19, 26:38](https://youtu.be/UdsLO5OQ6b8?t=1598)). In a 4H uptrend the big green dot itself is "very powerful to say it's time to go into a long" ([2 Powerful Scalping Tips, 2021-08-19, 26:07](https://youtu.be/UdsLO5OQ6b8?t=1567)).
- **Trigger invalidation.** For a bearish trigger, "as long as we don't print a momentum wave above this one here it's still a bearish trigger wave" ([Divergence Masterclass, 2025-05-21, 41:29](https://youtu.be/11pOQcHI9SU?t=2489)). By mirror symmetry (his symmetry, not his words), a bullish trigger is void once a new momentum trough prints below the anchor's trough.
- **Ambiguous cases.** He sometimes declines a pattern: "you might be able to say that this was an anchor then a trigger but I wouldn't consider that" ([4/24 Beginner Strategy, 2024-02-06, 10:11](https://youtu.be/eIJBiUakLN0?t=611)). On 2021-03-16 he said "whether or not this is an anchor or a trigger was still up for debate" ([How to Find the Best Trades, 2021-03-18, 29:36](https://youtu.be/Ma2lR62m4nA?t=1776)–[How to Find the Best Trades, 2021-03-18, 30:06](https://youtu.be/Ma2lR62m4nA?t=1806)). Trigger identification is partly by eye.
- **Time between anchor and trigger: nothing stated.** No bar count or window appears in any of the 17 transcripts.

### What he waits for after the dot prints (strategy by strategy)
1. **4/24 (2021 version, five conditions).** (a) a trigger wave on 4H MC B; (b) Wolf Pack ID crosses zero or changes colour on the 4H; (c) Wolf Pack ID crosses zero on the 24m; then (d) **whichever comes first**: a 24m green trigger wave, or a 24m money flow cross from red to green ([How to Find the Best Trades, 2021-03-18, 20:27](https://youtu.be/Ma2lR62m4nA?t=1227)–[How to Find the Best Trades, 2021-03-18, 21:29](https://youtu.be/Ma2lR62m4nA?t=1289); [How to Find the Best Trades, 2021-03-18, 42:59](https://youtu.be/Ma2lR62m4nA?t=2579)). ### How a dot is confirmed or invalidated
- **Confirmed = the candle closes with the dot on it.** "this red dot is not permanently in history until this one hour candle closes" ([High Win Rate Beginner Strategy, 2021-09-07, 21:21](https://youtu.be/hWNA0jOoRsc?t=1281)). "a divergence is confirmed when a candle closes and on market cipher a green dot is now printed forever" ([Divergence Masterclass, 2025-05-21, 09:16](https://youtu.be/11pOQcHI9SU?t=556)). A dot confirmed on a higher timeframe "is a very strong signal that we're going to get continued upside" ([Divergence Masterclass, 2025-05-21, 10:20](https://youtu.be/11pOQcHI9SU?t=620)). On the 24m, "as it confirms right that means the candle closes" ([4/24 Beginner Strategy, 2024-02-06, 07:06](https://youtu.be/eIJBiUakLN0?t=426)).
- **Invalidated before the close.** The dot disappears if price reverses inside the bar. For a red dot: "if price gets another pump, this red dot will disappear, and the wave won't cross over" ([High Win Rate Beginner Strategy, 2021-09-07, 21:55](https://youtu.be/hWNA0jOoRsc?t=1315); [High Win Rate Beginner Strategy, 2021-09-07, 23:35](https://youtu.be/hWNA0jOoRsc?t=1415)). For a green dot: if the candle "had come all the way down to make a lower low this green dot would not have stayed on the chart" ([Divergence Masterclass, 2025-05-21, 09:49](https://youtu.be/11pOQcHI9SU?t=589)).
- **The trap without lower-timeframe confirmation.** "we actually do print a red dot but then we print a green dot". He avoids this by checking the lower timeframes ([Finding PERFECT Entries, 2021-04-10, 13:37](https://youtu.be/EwtsVvxt30g?t=817)). Another failure mode is a red dot, then a green dot above zero, then another pump. That is why he is careful shorting "if you're not at the exact top" ([Finding PERFECT Entries, 2021-04-10, 22:52](https://youtu.be/EwtsVvxt30g?t=1372)).
- **Setup invalidation is price-based.** For a long: a lower low below the last swing low. For a short: a higher high above the last swing high ([High Win Rate Beginner Strategy, 2021-09-07, 24:38](https://youtu.be/hWNA0jOoRsc?t=1478)–[High Win Rate Beginner Strategy, 2021-09-07, 25:11](https://youtu.be/hWNA0jOoRsc?t=1511); [How to not Lose Trades, 2021-04-28, 08:51](https://youtu.be/sFA5iHDlPq8?t=531)). For the trigger wave, see "Trigger invalidation" above ([Divergence Masterclass, 2025-05-21, 41:29](https://youtu.be/11pOQcHI9SU?t=2489)).

## Candidate rules NOT in the pre-registered setup grader
1. **Wait for the trigger wave after the dot (daily translation of "don't long the anchor wave, long the trigger wave").** After a daily Buy dot (WT2 ≤ −60), call the trough of WT1 around it the anchor. ## Not usable / vague
- **"Synergistic properties".** His core method is to find your own personal combination of conditions by eyeballing the past and backtesting it. It is a process, not a rule ([How to Find the Best Trades, 2021-03-18, 05:42](https://youtu.be/Ma2lR62m4nA?t=342)–[How to Find the Best Trades, 2021-03-18, 11:49](https://youtu.be/Ma2lR62m4nA?t=709)).
- **"Clear direction", "pointy" waves, "curving over", "tight" trigger wave, "cluster of triggers".** These are eyeball judgements with no thresholds ([Finding PERFECT Entries, 2021-04-10, 14:40](https://youtu.be/EwtsVvxt30g?t=880); [High Win Rate Beginner Strategy, 2021-09-07, 20:50](https://youtu.be/hWNA0jOoRsc?t=1250); [How to Find the Best Trades, 2021-03-18, 52:21](https://youtu.be/Ma2lR62m4nA?t=3141)).

- **Wolf Pack ID, Crown RSI EMA, Market Cipher A diamonds.** These are not in the terminal. Wolf Pack ID's formula is not given.
- **Levels:** hand-drawn zones, value area, POC and naked POC, liquidity "boxes". Not computable without a volume-profile implementation and his choices. Horizontals partly overlap with the grader's body pivots.
- **"Something special" about the 12m and 24m.** No reason is given; an empirical claim at most.
- **The market-maker narrative and "what wrecks the most people".** Interpretive, not a rule.
- **The 365-day / 0.786 cycle bottom.** Only three prior instances, and it borders on the cycle school's domain. It is his stated claim, recorded under calibration, not proposed as a rule here. As a timing rule it would rest on n = 3.
- **Personal results and P&L screenshots.** Unverifiable.
- **Advice that changed over time:** smallest timeframe (5m in 2021, 5s in 2023); waiting for the dot (yes for beginners, no for himself). These are not rules to combine. A test must pick one stated version.
