## Method in its own terms

Market Cipher B is read as three oscillators. The **yellow VWAP** is the fastest ("turbo"). The **blue momentum waves** are two lines, a light blue and a deep blue. A dot prints where they cross ("engine"). **Money flow** is a green/red area, the slowest ("liquid", "blood"). Price follows momentum and money flow when they point the same way. Money flow is "king" and momentum is the stronger short-term push. The channel's named setup is the **Blue Wave / core / basic strategy**. A fat, deep **anchor wave** (below the white line and the dotted line, ideally with a green dot) is followed by a **noticeably smaller trigger wave**. You enter at the moment the trigger wave's light blue turns dark blue, which is the trigger wave's cross. Only take it when money flow is moving in a **smooth, round, predictable curve**, even if it is still red. The best-documented version adds that a **higher timeframe must "back up"** the entry: its money flow, RSIs and momentum must not be falling. Exits are discretionary: hold while money flow keeps its smooth path, project when it will turn green, and leave on Market Cipher A warnings (red or blood diamonds) or the opposite wave turn. Market Cipher A symbols (yellow X, blood diamond), DBSI scores and Heikin-Ashi candles add "confluence". Beyond "set a stop" and "don't DCA a loser", risk rules come only from the guests (1 to 2% per trade, 2 to 3R).

## Anchor wave, trigger wave, money flow and confirmation (exact)
