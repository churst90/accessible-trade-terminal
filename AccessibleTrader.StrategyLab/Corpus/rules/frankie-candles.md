## Method in its own terms

MC B has two jobs. **Money flow** gives "the longer term trend" of whatever timeframe you are on, and works as "a filter into whether you should be looking for Longs or shorts". A money flow cross into green means expect the uptrend to continue. A cross into red means expect downtrend continuation, so sell rallies. On the **weekly**, the deep red money flow waves have been the places to dollar-cost-average spot for the next bull market. The **momentum waves** give reversal entries through **divergences**. The **red and green dots** are only "ebbs and flows", noise, not trend calls. He uses them to *confirm* a divergence once the dot has printed and confirmed. The **VWAP** zero cross anticipates the dot. The best case is a divergence that comes with a money flow cross in the same direction.

## Candidate rules NOT in the pre-registered setup grader

1. **Money flow zero cross as a trend-continuation filter.** On timeframe T, after MF crosses above 0, take only longs until it crosses back. The test: does the forward return over N bars differ by MF sign? Computable. The grader uses MF only as a slope/veto layer, not as a side filter.
2. **Weekly MF deep-red accumulation.** Buy spot (no stop) while weekly MF < 0 and below its own k-th percentile. Hold until the weekly MF crosses up and later peaks. Computable on weekly BTC, but n is tiny (3 to 4 cycles). Mark it low-power.
3. **Divergence + same-side MF cross.** A bullish regular divergence (terminal Cipher B divergences) whose confirming green dot comes within k bars of an MF up-cross. The grader has divergence within 5 bars as a confirmation but no MF zero-cross pairing. Computable.
4. **Higher-timeframe MF flip as the exit for a lower-timeframe counter-trend trade.** For example, exit a daily short when weekly MF crosses above 0. Computable.
