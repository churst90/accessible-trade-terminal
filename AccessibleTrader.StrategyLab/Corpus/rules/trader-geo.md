## Method in its own terms

Geo trades Market Cipher B, and he says over and over that "money flow is King". He gives three ways to use it. (1) **Two timeframes** (2023-06): pick an "environmental" timeframe (4H for swings, 1H for day trades, 15m for scalps). Wait for it to get oversold (a green dot near or below −60) or overbought. Then drop to an "execution" timeframe 5 to 10 times shorter (4H → 24m) and wait for divergences, above all money flow divergence, and the green dot to close. (2) The **"cheat sheet entry"** (2023-09), a lower-timeframe long (he likes 3m). It has four conditions: thick green money flow, a green dot below the zero line, price pulling back into the 50 EMA (ideally on red or pink vector candles), and a hidden bullish divergence. Only the first two are "non-negotiable". The stop goes under the low, the first target is the high of the move, and he exits on the first bearish divergence on the entry timeframe. (3) **Key levels first** (2024-11): "I put Market Cipher away until I'm at a key level". He ignores divergences and money flow between levels, then reads MC B across timeframes once price reaches a level. Risk is talked about but never given a number.

## Anchor wave, trigger wave, money flow and confirmation (exact)

**He never uses the words "anchor wave" or "trigger wave"** in these three videos. He talks about "humps" (waves) and divergences between them. What he does say about each item:

- **Anchor wave (depth).** Nothing stated under that name. The closest rules:
  - Two-timeframe strategy: on the environmental timeframe a green dot "relatively close to or lower than this 60 line" means look for longs, and a red dot above the 60 line means look for shorts ([EASY Market Cipher Strategy, 2023-06-02, 02:37](https://youtu.be/2QGTGSXjHi4?t=157)). You wait for the environmental timeframe to be "oversold which is below or close to this line" ([EASY Market Cipher Strategy, 2023-06-02, 03:07](https://youtu.be/2QGTGSXjHi4?t=187)). So the depth is "at or near −60", with no number for "near".
  - Cheat sheet entry: the dot only has to be **below the zero line**, not −60. "the deeper it is while money flow remains thick the stronger of a cheat sheet entry" ("like a trampoline") ([Cheat Sheet Entry, 2023-09-08, 02:01](https://youtu.be/qC9JMB4nmHI?t=121); [Cheat Sheet Entry, 2023-09-08, 02:32](https://youtu.be/qC9JMB4nmHI?t=152)).

- **Trigger wave.** Nothing stated under that name. ## Entry triggers

- **Two-timeframe long.** Environmental timeframe at or near −60 with a green dot → on the execution timeframe, a bullish divergence (oscillator higher low, price lower low) with money flow crossing up → enter after the green dot closes ([EASY Market Cipher Strategy, 2023-06-02, 02:37](https://youtu.be/2QGTGSXjHi4?t=157); [EASY Market Cipher Strategy, 2023-06-02, 05:11](https://youtu.be/2QGTGSXjHi4?t=311)). Shorts mirror it: a red dot above 60, then bearish divergences on the execution timeframe ([EASY Market Cipher Strategy, 2023-06-02, 05:42](https://youtu.be/2QGTGSXjHi4?t=342)).
- **Cheat sheet long, four conditions** ([Cheat Sheet Entry, 2023-09-08, 01:01](https://youtu.be/qC9JMB4nmHI?t=61) to [Cheat Sheet Entry, 2023-09-08, 05:06](https://youtu.be/qC9JMB4nmHI?t=306)):
  1. thick green money flow;
  2. green dot below the MC B zero line;
  3. price retracing into the 50 EMA, preferably with red or pink (bearish) vector candles;
  4. hidden bullish divergence with momentum, money flow and/or VWAP.
  - "the only two that are absolutely non-negotiable is the money flow must be green and the Green Dot must be below the zero line" ([Cheat Sheet Entry, 2023-09-08, 10:12](https://youtu.be/qC9JMB4nmHI?t=612)).
  - But: "I probably wouldn't even enter if I didn't see one two and three". The fourth "is really kind of up in the air" ([Cheat Sheet Entry, 2023-09-08, 10:43](https://youtu.be/qC9JMB4nmHI?t=643)). **In practice 1 to 3 are required and 4 is optional.**
  - Shorts are "the complete opposite" ([Cheat Sheet Entry, 2023-09-08, 01:01](https://youtu.be/qC9JMB4nmHI?t=61)).
- **Key-level entry.** At the level, look for MC B "a certain way" across timeframes. His short example used multiple wave divergences at the level ("red white red white") plus failing structure. He built a small position first, then added "a little bit heavier" ([The Two Ways To Use Market Cipher B, 2024-11-01, 04:04](https://youtu.be/Uril5gdDpO0?t=244); [The Two Ways To Use Market Cipher B, 2024-11-01, 04:36](https://youtu.be/Uril5gdDpO0?t=276)). - **Money flow not green, or the dot not below zero** → "it is not a cheat sheet entry no way".
