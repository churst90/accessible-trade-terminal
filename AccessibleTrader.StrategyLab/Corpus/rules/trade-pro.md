## Method in its own terms

A trend-pullback system on Cipher B's blue waves. Use only the waves (dots = crosses) and money flow; switch off the VWAP, stochastic RSI and regular divergences. Four filters: price on the right side of the **200 EMA**; price **pulled back into the 50 EMA**; **money flow the colour of the trade** (green for longs); and the **blue waves on the opposite side of zero** (below zero for longs). With all four true, enter **on the blue wave cross** (the dot). Stop just beyond the swing, target **2R**. Shorts mirror all of it. In his 100-trade test (EUR/USD, 30m, about seven months), 54 trades won and 46 lost at 1:2.

## Candidate rules NOT in the pre-registered setup grader

1. **The Trade Pro system as a complete rule set (computable).** On bar t, a long fires when all of these hold:
   - close > EMA200;
   - within the last k bars, the low touched EMA50 or below (k is not stated; examples suggest the cross comes within a few bars of the touch);
   - money flow(t) > 0;
   - WT1 crosses up through WT2 on bar t;
   - WT2(t) < 0.

   Stop = the most recent swing low (pivot low, span not stated). Target = entry + 2 × (entry − stop). Mirror for shorts. **The grader differs on every layer.** It uses weekly context instead of EMA200, weekly pivots/EMA10 instead of EMA50, WT2 ≤ −60 instead of < 0, and has no MF > 0 requirement. Its fixed-R exits were tested in a different setting. Check the claimed 54%-at-2R directly: on EUR/USD 30m if FX data exists (needs data we may not have), and on the repo's daily crypto set otherwise. Terminal: Cipher B, EMA.
2. **Money flow sign as a hard filter** (MF > 0 for longs at the cross). The grader only vetoes MF < 0 AND falling. A one-layer ablation.
3. **Zero, not −60, as the depth gate.** Test whether WT2 < 0 versus WT2 ≤ −60 changes outcomes inside the same trend/pullback filter.
