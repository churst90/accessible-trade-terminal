# Asset Profiling Findings (round 12, 2026-06-12)

Generated by `StrategyLab asset-profile --snapshots ../strategy-lab-data`. Ten crypto
1d snapshots, walk-forward rolling windows, no-reverse, 10bps commission + 5bps slippage.
Each probe = a Cipher-B reversal setup; cell reports mean per-trade R / % of windows positive.

## The question this answers

"How do you analyze an asset, what carries across assets, and how do you account for an
asset's volatility changing as it matures?" The harness measures, per asset: volatility
**maturation** (early-history realized vol vs late-history), regime character (Hurst,
trend%, bull%), and the **long and short setup edge** for three signal families each.

## Threads (the load-bearing findings)

1. **Hurst does NOT discriminate between assets.** It reads 0.57–0.60 on every one of the
   ten — and mean-reversion% sits at ~8% everywhere. As a *cross-asset classifier it carries
   zero information here.* Drop it from asset analysis. The discriminators that actually spread
   are the **volatility maturation ratio** (0.51 → 1.37) and **bull%** (34% → 64%).

2. **`L:v23` is the universal long workhorse.** Positive mean (+0.19R) across all 10 assets,
   65% of windows positive, strong (>0.20R & ≥70%) on BCH, SOL, ETH, BTC. This is the
   deployable default long; everything else is an overlay or a specialization.

3. **`L:div` (bullish divergence) is the per-trade-R champion but liquidity-gated.** Highest
   mean of any probe (+0.37R) and fires only on the 5 deepest/most-liquid assets
   (SOL +0.75, LTC +0.61, ETH +0.37, TAO +0.14, BTC −0.02); too rare to evaluate on
   XRP/KAS/BCH/ADA/DOGE. Confirms rounds 11's conclusion a third time: high-quality, low-frequency,
   a conviction overlay for deep assets, not a standalone.

4. **`L:blue` (oversold crossover alone) has no standalone edge** (+0.04R, 46% positive).
   Re-confirms the long-standing finding — the blue dot only pays inside the OR-trigger or
   with divergence confluence, never alone.

5. **Shorts are NOT a mirror of longs — they are an asset-maturity phenomenon.** As a class
   shorts are the worst probes (S:red −0.11R, S:v23 −0.01R on average). But their wins
   *cluster on the young / still-expanding / post-pump assets* — TAO (S:red +0.25/100%,
   S:v23 +0.26/75%), DOGE (S:div +0.25/100%), ADA (S:div +0.60/50%), KAS (S:red +0.09/75%),
   XRP (S:red +0.11/71%) — and *uniformly fail on the deep majors* BTC/ETH/LTC (every short
   probe negative). The structural read: matured assets have a persistent institutional bid +
   upward drift that punishes shorts and rewards dip-buying; young/expanding assets are
   two-sided boom-bust where shorts find edge.

6. **`bull%` tracks long edge directionally.** BTC (64% bull) → strongest longs; ADA (34%),
   LTC (40%) → weakest/patchiest longs. Time-above-200MA is a cheap proxy for "does dip-buying
   pay on this asset."

## How to analyze an asset (the deliverable)

Compute two numbers from price history alone:
- **Vol maturation ratio** = realized vol (last third) / realized vol (first third).
- **Bull%** = fraction of bars with close > SMA200.

Then route:
- **Matured + bull-biased** (ratio < ~0.65, bull% > 50%, deep history) → **long-only**, `L:v23`
  as the base, add `L:div` if the asset is liquid enough to fire it. Don't short.
- **Young / expanding / post-pump** (ratio > ~0.75 or short history) → **enable shorts**
  (`S:v23`/`S:red`/`S:div`), treat longs as secondary.
- **SOL is the rare two-sided standout** (both L and S positive) — high vol, high liquidity,
  still maturing.

This is an argument for a **strategy-level asset classifier**, not a per-bar indicator: the
maturation ratio is computed once per asset and routes which strategy family runs. Building it
as an indicator would be redundant — it does not vary intra-asset on the timescale that matters.

## Honest caveats

- Young assets have only **4 rolling windows** — their short-edge results are *suggestive, not
  confirmed*. The matured-major long edge (BTC 14 win, ETH/XRP 7, BCH/LTC 6) is the firmer half.
- The vol ratio **conflates genuine maturation (BTC) with post-pump deflation (KAS)** — KAS's
  early 143% was a launch spike, so its "matured" label is misleading. Bar count + drift help
  disambiguate.
- All ten assets are **crypto**. Stocks (different drift/vol structure) need the Alpaca key —
  not currently reachable from the lab (the one stored Alpaca snapshot is a 1-bar failed pull).
  `StrategyLab snapshot --provider alpaca --symbol SPY --tf 1d --bars 3000 --key … --secret …`
  would add them.
